# IEA — Inner Emotional Architecture
*Positioning & Content Strategy — source of truth for this repo. Every article, draft, and idea here must align with this file.*

## What IEA Is
**IEA = Inner Emotional Architecture.** An original framework created by the author.

- IEA is **not coaching.**
- IEA is **not consulting.**
- IEA is **not mentorship.**
- IEA is **a way back to yourself.**

It maps the inner emotional architecture a person already has — the load-bearing
structures beneath their decisions, leadership, and identity — and offers a return
to the self underneath the roles.

## Positioning vs. the Crowded Field
The "personal development / coaching" lane on Medium is owned by big names
(habits, productivity, morning routines, stoicism, "unlock your potential").
IEA does **not** compete there. IEA competes in the gap between:

- **Psychology** (6.2M followers, best reach-per-story ratio ~17.7 on Medium)
- **Leadership** (3M followers)
- **Startup / Business / Entrepreneurship** (2.4M / 4.6M / 2.9M followers)
- **Mental Health** (5.7M followers)

...while speaking only to **founders and leaders**.

## The 3 Topic Lanes for Founders & Leaders

### Lane 1 — The Inner Architecture of Leadership
*Psychology × Leadership.* The leader's inner patterns become the company's
patterns: how unexamined emotional architecture caps decision quality, culture,
and scale. Engineering/systems metaphor native to founders.
Example headlines:
- "Your Company Can't Outgrow Your Inner Architecture"
- "The Load-Bearing Walls: What Founders Don't Know They're Carrying"
- "Every Company Has Its Founder's Emotional Blueprint — Here's How to Read Yours"

### Lane 2 — The Self Behind the Title
*Entrepreneurship × Mental Health.* What success does to identity: founder–
company fusion, the mask of the strong one, loneliness at the top, success and
emptiness. First-person transformation essays — Medium's most-boosted format.
Example headlines:
- "I Built the Company and Lost Myself — the Architecture of Founder Identity"
- "The Strongest Person in the Room Is Often the Most Absent"
- "What Happens When the Company Becomes Your Personality"

### Lane 3 — The Way Back
*IEA's signature arc.* Not optimization — return. Practical, IEA-grounded essays
on finding your way back to yourself after burnout, exit, failure, betrayal, or
scale. The anti-coaching frame: no one leads you back; the way back is inward.
Example headlines:
- "The Way Back: Why Founders Don't Need Coaches, They Need a Return"
- "After the Exit: the Homecoming No One Plans For"
- "Burnout Isn't a Fuel Problem. It's an Architecture Problem."
