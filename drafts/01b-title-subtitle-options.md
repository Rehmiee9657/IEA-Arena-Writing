## Recommendation
- **Publish with Option 1.** It's the only candidate that is simultaneously clear, curious, and searchable — and "personality" is the word readers screenshot.
- **Option 2 is your A/B swap** if performance lags (see protocol below). First-person + perfect symmetry; it just trades some search reach for emotional click.
- Keep **Option 4's title** in reserve for a follow-up essay about support/loneliness — it deserves its own piece rather than diluting this one.

## How to actually A/B on Medium
1. Publish with Option 1. In the first 24–48 h, watch **read ratio** (reads / views) more than views — this genre lives on completion, not clicks.
2. Decision gates:
   - Read ratio **≥ 60%** and climbing views → leave it.
   - Views fine, read ratio **< 55%** → title promised more than the opening delivered; swap the *subtitle* to Option 2's first.
   - Low views **and** low ratio after 72 h → swap full title to Option 2 and re-share to a different audience slice (e.g., the tags not yet exercised).
3. Never change title *and* first paragraph in the same edit — you'll lose the ability to tell which one moved the number.
4. Medium titles display fully up to ~60 characters; all four options are safe. Option 1 = 42 chars. ✓
